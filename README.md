most of what i build starts as an annoyance. a file i didn't want to upload to some stranger's server. an ai assistant that acted first and asked never. i fix it for myself, then leave it here in case it helps you too.

i'd like the things i make to be useful to someone i'll never meet. good tools should be free to the people who need them most, like a student without a budget, someone who can't use a mouse, or anyone who'd rather their files stayed their own.

almost everything i know, i learned for free from strangers on the internet. this is me paying it back :)
